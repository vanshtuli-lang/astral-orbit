# Nova Retail customer policy

## Returns
- Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery.
- Returned items must be unused and in the original packaging.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5-7 business days of us receiving the item.

## Damaged or faulty items
- Ask the customer for a photo of the damage.
- Once the damaged item is back with us, we send a replacement or a full refund, whichever the customer prefers.
- Return shipping for damaged items is free.

## Shipping
- Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days.
- Orders that haven't arrived 10 business days after shipping are investigated with the carrier.

## Customer tiers
- STANDARD: regular customers.
- GOLD: our loyalty members. They get free shipping on every order.
